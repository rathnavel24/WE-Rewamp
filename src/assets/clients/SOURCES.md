# Client logos

Logos for the Home "Trusted by" strip. Each file is named by the client's slug in `src/content/clients.ts`.

All 11 come from the client's own final logo sheet (13 November 2026), which also sets the list and its order.
Each was cut out of that sheet: the white page background made transparent (white inside a logo is kept), then
trimmed with a small margin. The strip always shows them in full colour.

Logos remain the property of their owners; the client has supplied them for this use.
