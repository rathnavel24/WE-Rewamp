## About the client

WalletEdge Global is a parent group headquartered in SPC Freezone, Sharjah (UAE), with an office in
Coimbatore (India). It operates through group companies across four sectors: IT & BPO, Financial
Services, Consulting and Healthcare.

**Audience, in priority order:**

1. Corporate clients and partners, especially insurers, brokers and property developers in the UAE/GCC.
2. Investors and stakeholders evaluating the group.
3. Patients and families looking for the healthcare businesses (home nursing, pharmacy, diagnostics).

**The site's job:** establish credibility as a serious, diversified group; explain clearly which
company delivers which service; and turn interest into enquiries.

## Brand and assets

- **Logo:** the existing WalletEdge logo (orange bars with a green "E" mark and the wordmark
  "WALLETEDGE"; tagline "IT & BPO | Financial Services | Consulting | Health Care"). Don't redraw it.
  Keep it clear and prominent in the header.
- **Colour:** build the palette around the logo's forest green and burnt orange. You may refine the
  supporting neutrals and accents.
- **Typography:** you're free to choose. Pick typefaces that suit a serious, GCC-facing corporate
  group, and explain why you chose them. Avoid the defaults every site uses (Inter, Roboto, Poppins,
  Montserrat).
- **Logos available:** all 15 client logos (`src/assets/clients/`), plus logos for CareHub, Global
  Cure RX, WhiteBlue, InsureLiv and ArogyaPlus (`src/assets/logos/`). The four Wallet Edge companies
  use the WalletEdge logo. Logos must sit cleanly on both light and dark backgrounds.

## Design direction

- **Professional and modern:** in the spirit of a top-tier consultancy or holding-group website, not
  a startup landing page.
- **Built for credibility:** clear hierarchy, generous whitespace, restrained colour and strong
  typography.
- **Avoid templated "AI-made" patterns:**
  - identical rounded cards for everything, and cards nested inside cards
  - gradient blobs and glows used as decoration
  - an uppercase label above every heading
  - numbered "01 / 02 / 03" markers on content that isn't a sequence
  - fade-up animations on every section
- **Motion:** at most one considered moment, such as the hero. Everything must respect
  reduced-motion settings.
- **Information design matters most:** a visitor should understand the group's structure within
  10 seconds (parent → services → companies → platforms).

## Quality bar

- Fully responsive from 360px phones to large desktops, with no horizontal scrolling.
- WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, visible keyboard focus, real alt text, semantic headings, 44px touch targets.
- Fast: real text rather than text baked into images, optimised images, and no heavy libraries
  without a reason.
- The canonical domain is https://walletedgeglobal.com. Include page titles, descriptions and
  share-preview tags.

## Technical context (for implementation)

React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + React Router + CSS Modules, with design tokens in CSS custom
properties. There is no UI library. Content lives in typed TypeScript files, separate from components.

## Deliverables

1. **Design rationale (short):** audience, positioning, palette (hex values), typefaces and their
   roles, layout system, and what makes this design specific to WalletEdge.
2. **Full designs** for every page above, at desktop and mobile widths, including hover, focus,
   empty and error states (the form's error and success states).
3. **A token sheet:** colour, type scale, spacing, radii and shadows.
4. **Content audit:** every place content is missing from the document, every assumption you made,
   and a confirmation that nothing was added beyond the document.

Before designing, briefly restate the brief and list any questions or conflicts you find in the
document, then proceed.
